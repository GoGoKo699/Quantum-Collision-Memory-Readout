# Scientific account

A probe can register environmental memory after a causal break because an earlier collision changed the bath. Here the middle measurement does something more specific. A flipped result gives the bath a known state. A second flip samples an output component of the intervening bath channel. Private randomization makes their joint occurrence rate independent of that channel, yielding calibrated conditional statistics.

1. [Model](model.md) defines the two-collision apparatus, promises and resources.
2. [Tomography](tomography.md) constructs the estimator and proves its finite-budget guarantees and blind-reset ambiguity.
3. [Detection](testing.md) establishes exact one-block information bounds and the matched weak-coupling detection laws.
4. [Readout noise](readout-noise.md) studies symmetric middle-report errors in the detection task.
5. [Calibrated tomography](calibrated-tomography.md) estimates the full affine map with a known symmetric middle-report error.

The [prior-work comparison](../references/RELATED_WORK.md) identifies the inherited causal-break and indirect-probe ingredients. The [verification script](../verification/README.md) checks the algebra against explicit matrix evolutions.
