# Related work

**Causal breaks and the inherited collision model.** Pollock and collaborators use two partial swaps with a maximally mixed bath in their supplemental Appendix B.2, Figure 2. Equations B2–B3 characterize the conditioned bath and subsequent probe. They use this to expose causal dependence even as snapshot distinguishability decreases. The present protocol uses a specific middle flag to prepare known bath inputs and a second, randomized flag to read the intervening map independently of the collision angles. [Pollock et al., PRL 120, 040405, Supplemental Material](https://journals.aps.org/prl/supplemental/10.1103/PhysRevLett.120.040405/20171215_PRL_supplemental.pdf)

**Conditional correlations and randomized resets.** Budini demonstrates time-ordered measurements with conditional correlations and postselection. In the randomized protocol of the later account, Equation (8) averages over the actual middle outcome before forming its statistic. Here that outcome flags the bath input. The final flag and private signs let the estimator recover each matrix element with its own calibrated population law. [Budini, PRL 121, 240401](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.121.240401) · [Budini, Entropy 24, 649, §§2.2–2.3](https://doi.org/10.3390/e24050649)

**Memory and tomography.** Salih treats a one-slot partial-SWAP process with depolarizing bath storage and an inverse second collision, studying its classical and quantum realizability boundary. The input dependence tested here can occur even for an entanglement-breaking map. The target is the intervening bath channel in a fixed factorization of the apparatus. Process-tensor tomography reconstructs a multi-time process on the accessible system, a different object. [Salih, preprint 202611.2176](https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202611.2176) · [White et al., PRX Quantum 3, 020344](https://doi.org/10.1103/PRXQuantum.3.020344)

**Operational comparison.** Liu and collaborators compare SWAP-like reuse and reinitialization for metrological precision. Estimating another dynamical parameter under those configurations and identifying a hidden channel with drifting exchange have different inference targets. [Liu et al., Advanced Quantum Technologies 7, 2400094, §5.2](https://doi.org/10.1002/qute.202400094)
